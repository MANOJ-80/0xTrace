# 0xTrace

Interview experience tracker — scraped and compiled from [InterviewShare](https://interview-share.vercel.app/) for PSG College of Technology students.

## Contents

| File | Company | Experiences | Rounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Societe_Generale_Interview_Experiences.md` | Societe Generale | 2 | 3-4 rounds each |
| `Birla_Pivot_Interview_Experiences.md` | Birla Pivot | 2 | 5 rounds each |

## What's Inside

Each document contains:
- **Structured metadata** — company, role, date, rating, stipend, location
- **Round-by-round breakdown** — duration, platform, difficulty, actual questions asked
- **Coding problems** — with examples, topics, and solution approaches
- **Preparation tips & mistakes to avoid**
- **Comparative summary** across candidates

## Source

All experiences were scraped from [InterviewShare](https://interview-share.vercel.app/) — a platform by PSG College of Technology students sharing authentic interview experiences.
